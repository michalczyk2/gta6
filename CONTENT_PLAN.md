# Content plan @gta_6_daily_news

Goal: every post should stop the scroll. Do that with a question, a surprise or a comparison, not a dry news label.

## Weekly rotation (content pillars)

| Day | Pillar | Example covers (hook) |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Did you know?** Fun facts about the game, Leonida, the real Florida/Miami behind it | "Leonida is based on a REAL state 🐊", "This GTA 6 detail took 10 years" |
| Tue | **Comparisons**: GTA 6 vs GTA 5 / Vice City 2002 / real Miami / other games / everyday things (price, time, size) | "GTA 6 vs GTA 5: what changed?", "$80 = how many pizzas? 🍕" |
| Wed | **Game features**: one confirmed feature explained simply (characters, map, weather, wildlife, vehicles, social media in-game…) | "Your phone in GTA 6 does WHAT?", "Meet Jason & Lucia" |
| Thu | **News day**: Rockstar usually posts on Thursdays; recap of the week's news | "Rockstar just dropped…", BREAKING when official |
| Fri | **Question / debate**: would you rather, hot take, poll, ranking. Engagement post | "Would you rather play as Jason or Lucia?", "Day 1 or wait for reviews?" |
| Sat | **Top 5 / lists**: things to do first, easter eggs we expect, best GTA moments | "5 things I'll do first in GTA 6" |
| Sun | **Myth vs fact / rumor check**: what's real, what's fake, what's leaked | "Fake or real? 5 GTA 6 rumors checked ✅❌" |

Big official news always overrides the plan for that day.

## Hooks that work
- A question the viewer wants answered: "Why does GTA 6 cost $80?"
- A surprise number: "13 years. 3 console generations. 1 game."
- A comparison: "GTA 6 map vs GTA 5 map"
- A "you" angle: "What would YOU do first?"
- Curiosity gap: "Nobody noticed this in the trailer…" (only if true and sourced)

## Readability rules
- Cover title: max 6–7 words, one idea.
- Content slide: 1 title + max 2 short sentences (≤ 20 words) + 1 punchline.
- One fact per slide. If it doesn't fit, split it into two slides.
- Numbers as big "stat" slides; comparisons as "versus" slides; rankings as "list" slides.
- Every claim needs a source. Rumors are labelled "RUMOR".
